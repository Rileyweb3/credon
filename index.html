GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity is part
of the infrastructure.

Understand the concept, the proposed approach and the risks that come with trade financing.

What is CREDON?+

CREDON is an independent project exploring decentralized trade-finance infrastructure to connect verified businesses seeking funding with global capital providers.

What problem is CREDON trying to solve?+

Many small and medium-sized businesses face barriers to trade finance, including limited collateral, high costs, strict requirements and lengthy approval processes.

How would trade financing work?+

In the proposed model, businesses submit trade requests, relevant information is independently reviewed, and eligible opportunities may be assessed by potential capital providers under agreed terms.

Who can use CREDON?+

The intended ecosystem includes businesses seeking trade finance and eligible capital providers. Access, eligibility and availability have not yet been established.

How would businesses and trades be verified?+

Verification is envisioned as an independent process covering business identity, supplier information and relevant trade documents. The process and its providers are still to be defined.

How can capital providers participate?+

Potential capital providers may be able to assess eligible opportunities through a proposed framework. Participation terms, access and regulatory requirements are not yet announced.

What role do stablecoins play?+

Stablecoins may be explored as a mechanism for financing and settlement. Any implementation would depend on technical, legal and regulatory considerations.

What are smart contracts used for?+

Smart contracts can structure agreed terms and record transactions. They cannot independently verify physical-world events such as shipment, delivery or product quality.

Does CREDON guarantee financing returns?+

No. CREDON does not guarantee financing, repayment or returns. Trade financing carries commercial risks, including supplier fraud, shipment failure, delivery disputes and business default.

When will the platform launch?+

There is no confirmed launch date. Platform and token release details will be announced as the project progresses.
